Local Students Build Weather App

14 October 2026

A group of college students has built a simple weather app that shows the forecast for their town. The project took two weeks and was made entirely with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

The team says the hardest part was fixing small bugs, such as missing tags and wrong file names. They plan to add a seven-day forecast next month.
