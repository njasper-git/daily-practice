Masala Omlette

A delicious and spicy omelette packed with masala flavors by Jasper N, October 12, 2026

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp masala powder
  • Salt to taste
  • water

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs in a bowl.
  2. Add the masala powder and salt.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and pour the egg mixture.
  4. Cook until set and serve hot.
  5. serve hot
